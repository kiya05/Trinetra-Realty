Jindal Global City Sonipat is a modern integrated township planned across more than 214 acres.

Located in the growth corridor of Delhi/NCR, the township brings together residential plots, homes, open spaces, infrastructure and everyday conveniences within one thoughtfully planned community.

With plots ranging from approximately 156 to 781 sq. yd., residents have the flexibility to create homes suited to their lifestyle.
