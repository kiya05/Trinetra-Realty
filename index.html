About Trinetra Realty

Real estate advice built on trust & experience.

Established in 2014, Trinetra Realty is a trusted real estate consulting and investment advisory firm helping homebuyers, investors and NRIs discover the right property opportunities across Delhi NCR, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand.

For over a decade, we have helped homebuyers, investors and NRIs identify high-potential real estate opportunities through market intelligence, location research and strategic guidance.

We don’t simply facilitate property transactions. We build long-term relationships based on trust, transparency and results.

Market Intelligence Data-driven insights to identify the right locations and opportunities.
Strategic Advisory Property recommendations aligned with your goals, budget and investment horizon.
Trusted PartnershipsAccess to leading developers and selected inventory across North India.
End-to-End SupportFrom property selection and purchase to resale, exit planning and portfolio strategy.
