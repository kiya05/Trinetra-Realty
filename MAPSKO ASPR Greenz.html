Project Overview

Designed around your lifestyle.

ASPR Greenz is a plotted residential development in Sector 35, Sonipat, created for homeowners looking for the freedom and flexibility of independent living.

The development brings together planned internal roads, landscaped green spaces, lifestyle amenities and convenient connectivity in one residential environment.
