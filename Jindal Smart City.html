The Community

Modern living, thoughtfully planned.

Jindal Smart City is an integrated residential development at Sector 34, Sonipat, spread across approximately 95 acres.

The community brings together high-quality plotted development, luxury villas and built-up residences within a master-planned neighbourhood designed around greenery, recreation and convenience.

With residential, recreational, commercial and social infrastructure planned together, the development is designed to create a complete lifestyle rather than simply another residential address.
