Overview

The charm of Tuscan living, in Sonipat.

Eldeco Amaya brings the charm of Tuscan living to Sector 33, Sonipat, Haryana. Spread across 14 acres of serene landscapes, the community is planned around elegance, comfort and natural beauty.

The project offers villas and residential plots within a thoughtfully designed gated environment, with lifestyle amenities, landscaped greens and strong connectivity to key destinations around Sonipat and Delhi NCR.
